Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

About the app

Pussycat Defcall is a privately operated Discord bot for coordinating Travian gameplay, including defensive calls, resource push requests, and scouting.

Information processed

Depending on the features used, the bot may process Discord user and server identifiers, interaction details, resource reports, and Travian-related game information submitted by users.

Google Sheets access

The operator connects a Google account to allow the bot to read and update Google Sheets used for resource push coordination. The requested Google Sheets permission may technically allow access to spreadsheets available to the authorized account; the bot is intended to use this access for its configured coordination spreadsheets, not unrelated personal documents.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to operate bot commands, coordinate gameplay, and maintain related records. Relevant coordination information may be visible to members of the Discord server and to people with access to the connected spreadsheet. Google and Discord process information under their own terms and policies.

Storage and retention

The bot may retain operational data in its hosting environment and in connected Google Sheets. Records may remain until removed by the operator. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised.

Security and access

Access is managed through Discord permissions, hosting controls, and Google account or spreadsheet sharing settings. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Requests and contact

For questions about data access, correction, or deletion, contact the operator at pussycatdefcall@gmail.com. You can revoke the app's Google access through your Google Account settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data handling changes.
